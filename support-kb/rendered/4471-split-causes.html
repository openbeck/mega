Shared parser: day and month reversed

The shared parser reads month first. For tenants whose locale writes the day first, dates can flip. This is the larger group: app, email, and booking-v2 form reports across the day-first tenant directory.

Filed as openbeck/mega#1 · 379 reports. Engineering fixed this cause with fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales; the issue is closed as completed.

Booking-v3 hint: displayed order disagrees with tenant parsing

Booking-v3 displays MM/DD/YYYY. In the three affected day-first tenants, people follow that hint while the form interprets the date using the tenant's locale. Keep this as a separate issue from the shared parser: the form's displayed instruction is the cause.
